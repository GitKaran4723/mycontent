# Introduction to Logic and Propositional Logic

## 1. Introduction to Logic

Logic is the study of **reasoning and valid arguments**. It provides rules and methods to determine whether a given argument is **correct or incorrect**. In discrete mathematics, logic is used as a formal language to express mathematical ideas clearly and unambiguously.

Logic plays a crucial role in:

* Mathematical proofs
* Computer programming
* Algorithm design
* Digital circuits
* Artificial intelligence

In simple terms, logic helps us decide **what is true, what is false, and why**.

---

## 2. Meaning of Propositional Logic

**Propositional logic** is a branch of logic that deals with **propositions** and their relationships. It studies how propositions can be combined using logical operators and how the truth of compound statements depends on the truth of individual propositions.

In propositional logic:

* Each statement has a definite truth value (True or False)
* The internal structure of the statement is not analyzed
* Only the truth values matter

---

## 3. Proposition (Statement)

A **proposition** is a declarative sentence that is either **true or false**, but not both.

### Examples:

* “4 is an even number” → True
* “9 is a prime number” → False

### Not Propositions:

* “Open the door” (command)
* “What is your name?” (question)
* “x > 5” (depends on x)

Propositions are usually represented using symbols such as **p, q, r, s**.

---

## 4. Types of Propositions

### 4.1 Simple (Atomic) Proposition

A **simple proposition** is a proposition that does not contain any other proposition as a part.

**Example:**
p: “It is raining”

---

### 4.2 Compound Proposition

A **compound proposition** is formed by combining two or more simple propositions using logical connectives.

**Example:**
p: “It is raining”
q: “The road is wet”

Compound proposition:
p ∧ q → “It is raining and the road is wet”

---



##  Importance of Propositional Logic

Propositional logic is important because it:

* Forms the basis for predicate logic
* Helps in constructing mathematical proofs
* Is widely used in computer science and engineering
* Allows logical reasoning in a precise and systematic way
